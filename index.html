Contour drawing

Index contours remain continuous. Fine lines bridge short dense crossings, but sustained dense sections are trimmed. Tiny resulting fragments are removed by minimum contour length.

3D print export

STL dimensions follow the selected paper shape. Contour widths use the fine and coarse pen settings, with the nozzle diameter as their minimum printable width. Fine and index contours may use different heights; the backed export adds a 1.00 mm rectangular base.

The A1 package follows the selected paper shape when it fits the 256 mm bed. It is already sliced for a 0.40 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, a 1.00 mm white base, and black contours. Open it in Bambu Studio and print the sliced plate; do not slice it again.

The ordered plan stores true centerlines in millimetres: all fine contours first, index contours second, then the zero/coast boundary. Paths are spatially ordered and reversed where useful to reduce travel. It is the input for an exact G-code / sliced-3MF wrapper; it is not standalone printer G-code.
